# Pet Wellbeing & Veterinary Network — Product Roadmap

## Product promise
Help pet owners understand and improve everyday animal wellbeing and find appropriately qualified veterinary professionals, without replacing veterinary care.

## Strategic constraint
Zero upfront investment. Start with static hosting and public educational content. Do not promise working registration, verification, private records, payments or video consultations until the required backend and legal workflows exist.

## Information architecture
1. Home — mission, pet care pathways, featured guides, tools, vet network CTA, trust principles.
2. Dog Care — nutrition, healthy weight, behaviour, exercise/enrichment, dental care, life stages, preventive care.
3. Cat Care — environment, litter habits, enrichment, nutrition, dental care, life stages, preventive care.
4. Pet Tools — carefully validated educational tools; no drug dose calculators or AI symptom diagnosis.
5. Pet Wellbeing Journal — article categories, author/reviewer information, update dates and sources.
6. Find a Veterinarian — search by country/jurisdiction, location, species, specialty, language and service eligibility.
7. Join as a Veterinarian — self-service application, identity/credential review, profile management.
8. About & Standards — welfare-first promise, verification policy, privacy, clinical boundaries, corrections/complaints.
9. Later: My Pet / health passport, secure bookings, payments, teleconsultations, follow-up records.

## Phase 1 — Public validation (weeks 1–4 after setup)
- Choose and clear a brand name before buying a domain.
- Deploy to free GitHub Pages.
- Replace placeholder sitemap URLs and verify search indexing.
- Vet-review initial articles and calculator or label calculator as non-public demo until approved.
- Publish 8–12 genuinely useful articles based on search intent; no mass-produced generic pages.
- Connect privacy-safe analytics and Search Console if appropriate.
- Success measures: indexed pages, organic impressions/clicks, returning users, meaningful directory interest.

## Phase 2 — Directory backend (only after early interest)
- Select a free-tier backend with authentication, database and access control.
- Design data model: professional, regulator/jurisdiction, credential check, public profile, specialties, languages, clinic/service areas, availability, status history, complaints/suspensions.
- Secure registration form and verification queue; keep uploaded documents private.
- Public directory shows only approved profiles; label verification scope and last checked date.
- Add anti-spam, duplicate detection, email verification, privacy notice, deletion/correction requests and admin audit logs.
- Never auto-verify a professional based on self-declared information alone.

## Phase 3 — Consultation pilot
- Select one launch jurisdiction based on actual demand and regulatory feasibility.
- Obtain local legal/professional review of VCPR/telemedicine, prescribing, records, privacy, insurance, emergency follow-up and consumer terms.
- Confirm payment provider supports a Pakistan-based platform and provider payouts in the target country.
- Launch booking/payment/video only after all checks are complete; do not assume a video call can legally provide every type of care.
- Start with non-emergency services allowed by local rules; route emergencies to local in-person care.

## Phase 4 — Sustainable revenue
- Free basic directory listings to establish supply.
- Premium profiles/clinic plans only after the platform can demonstrate qualified leads.
- Consultation commission only after payment, refunds, disputes, payouts and clinical obligations are operational.
- Relevant advertising/affiliate links only when audience and content quality justify them; disclose commercial relationships.

## Clinical and trust red lines
- No fake diagnosis or AI symptom checker presented as a veterinarian.
- No medication-dose calculators in the initial product.
- No fabricated veterinary profiles or fake testimonials.
- No public display of private clinical records or credential documents.
- No paid clinical bookings across jurisdictions without eligibility checks.
- No misleading use of "verified" or "veterinary reviewed" labels.
- Corrections, sources, review dates and responsible editorial ownership for health content.

## First execution checklist
1. Inspect and test this prototype locally on desktop and mobile.
2. Select the final brand name (temporary name is Pet Wellbeing).
3. Create a GitHub repository and publish free static MVP.
4. Replace placeholder URLs and set up Search Console.
5. Vet-review the first articles and tool formula before promoting them as guidance.
6. Connect a secure vet interest form only after privacy and storage are configured.
