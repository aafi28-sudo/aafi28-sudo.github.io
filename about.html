Our promise

Animal wellbeing first.
Trust always.

Pet Wellbeing World is being built to make useful care knowledge easier to access and to help owners connect with appropriate veterinary professionals.

♡

Welfare before revenue

Commercial decisions must not encourage unnecessary treatment, fear-based purchasing or animal-welfare compromises.

✓

Transparent professional verification

We will distinguish self-submitted information from credentials independently checked. A verified status must have a defined process, scope and review date.

＋

Education is not diagnosis

Articles and tools provide general education. They do not diagnose disease or replace a clinical examination, individualized veterinary advice or emergency care.

⌑

Privacy by design

We will collect only necessary information, restrict access to sensitive records, explain how data is used and avoid exposing private medical information in public profiles.

Prototype status: This website is not yet a live veterinary marketplace. No clinical consultations, verified profiles, secure accounts or data submission services are active.
