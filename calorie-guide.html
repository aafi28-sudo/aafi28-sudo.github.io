How to use this safely

One number cannot describe a pet's needs.

Energy requirements vary with body condition, neuter status, activity, growth, pregnancy, environment, health and food composition. A generic multiplier can be substantially wrong for an individual animal.

01

Confirm the goal

Ask a veterinarian to assess body condition and determine whether weight maintenance, growth or a supervised change is appropriate.

02

Use the food label carefully

Energy density differs between foods. Grams or cups cannot be inferred from calories without the product's kcal information.

03

Monitor and adjust professionally

Track weight and body condition over time and seek guidance before making substantial changes.
